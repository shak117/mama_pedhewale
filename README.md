# Mama Pedhewale (मामा पेढेवाले) — E-Commerce Platform

An authentic, modern e-commerce web application for **Mama Pedhewale**, the iconic Satara sweet brand renowned for handcrafted **Satara Special Kandi Pedha**, pure desi ghee laddus, and royal gift assortments since 1948.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Heritage Royal Design & Marathi/English Branding**:
   - Royal palette: Saffron, maroon, gold, and warm cream.
   - Devanagari typography paired with modern responsive layouts.
   - Purity assurances: 100% Pure Buffalo Mawa, Shuddh Desi Ghee, Zero Additives.

2. **Interactive "Build Your Own Mithai Box" (Custom Assortment)**:
   - Live 4-compartment customizable sweet box builder.
   - Choose between **500g** and **1kg** sizes.
   - Mix-and-match sweets (Kandi Pedha, Kaju Katli, Motichoor Laddu, Kesar Malai, etc.).
   - Live visual box preview and weighted price calculation.

3. **Dynamic Weight Packs**:
   - Seamless toggling between **250g, 500g, and 1kg** on product cards and detail pages.
   - Automatic price recalculation and savings tags.

4. **Cart, Delivery & Checkout**:
   - Slide-over quick cart drawer with Free Shipping progress bar (Free shipping on orders above ₹799).
   - Real-time **PIN Code Serviceability & Speed Checker** (Same-Day local delivery in Satara/Pune vs. Express Courier).
   - Auspicious Delivery Date & Time Slot Scheduler (ideal for festivals, weddings, and pujas).
   - Free personalized gift message card.

5. **Payment Modes & WhatsApp Integration**:
   - **Simulated Instant UPI Payment**: Dynamic QR code for PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, and BHIM UPI.
   - Cash on Delivery (COD) and Net Banking.
   - **Direct WhatsApp Ordering**: One-click ordering that formats your entire cart into a pre-filled WhatsApp message sent directly to the store helpline.

6. **Order Tracking & Tax Invoice**:
   - Unique Order ID generation (`MP-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX`).
   - Live 4-stage fulfillment timeline (*Confirmed -> Fresh Packing -> Dispatched -> Delivered*).
   - Printable official tax invoice with FSSAI certification details.

7. **Corporate Gifting & Bulk Order Portal**:
   - Interactive bulk pricing calculator with tiered volume discounts (up to 20% off).
   - Bulk inquiry submission form with tasting box requests.

8. **Admin & Fulfillment Dashboard (`/admin`)**:
   - Live statistics: Total Orders, Revenue, Pending Kitchen Dispatch, Active Products.
   - Order fulfillment status management (*Confirmed, Packed, Dispatched, Delivered, Cancelled*).
   - Instant 1-click **In Stock / Out of Stock** inventory toggling.
