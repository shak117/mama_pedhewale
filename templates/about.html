The Legacy of Mama Pedhewale

Seven decades of handcrafting Maharashtra's most celebrated Kandi Pedha using the timeless philosophy of pure buffalo mawa, patient wood-fire reduction, and uncompromising devotion.

1948 • The Humble Beginning

Born at the historic foothills of Ajinkyatara Fort

In 1948, right after India gained independence, our founder lovingly known throughout Satara as "Mama" set up a small sweet counter near Shivaji Circle. Using rich, nutrient-dense buffalo milk sourced directly from local pastoral farmers in the Krishna-Venna river basin, Mama perfected the technique of slow-roasting mawa.

While commercial sweet shops cut corners with maida, flour, or milk powder, Mama stayed stubbornly committed to 100% pure mawa. The pedhas were so rich and fragrant that travelers across Maharashtra made a ritual stop at Satara just to carry home a box of Mama's pedhas.

Handcrafted in Copper Kadhais: The distinctive amber-brown hue is achieved naturally through 8 hours of gentle simmering.
