Our Heritage Story

Preserving the Legacy of Legendary Satari Pedha Since 1948

Satara has been the birthplace of India's most cherished caramelized pedhas. At Mama Pedhewale, we honor this 78-year-old culinary legacy by never rushing the process. Our khoya is simmered gently for over 6 to 8 hours until it develops its rich chocolate-brown hue naturally.

Zero Additives & No Preservatives: Prepared purely with khoya, minimal sugar, and organic spices.

Direct From Local Satara Dairies: Milk sourced fresh every morning from trusted local dairy farmers.

Vacuum Sealed Freshness: Multi-layer hygienic food-grade packaging ensures original moisture and aroma retainment across transit.
